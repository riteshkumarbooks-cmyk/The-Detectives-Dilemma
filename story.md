EPISODE 1: SHADOWS AND SILK
TEASER
SCENE 1
INT. LLOYD SCHWARZER’S OFFICE - LATE AFTERNOON
The office is a testament to faded glory. Dust motes dance in the shafts of amber light slicing through the venetian blinds. The shadows on the floor look like prison bars.
LLOYD SCHWARZER (30s, handsome in a jagged way, wearing a rumpled suit) sits with his feet on the desk, tossing a tennis ball against the wall. Thud. Thud.
Sitting atop a stack of unpaid bills is LUNA, a tabby cat with eyes too intelligent for an animal. She licks a paw dismissively.
LUNA > You’re staring at the phone again. It’s pathetic. It’s a piece of plastic, Lloyd, not a lover waiting to apologize.
Lloyd doesn’t blink. He catches the ball.
LLOYD > It’s not the phone, Luna. It’s the silence. The rent is due on Tuesday, and my bank account is currently echoing.LUNA > (Yawns) > Perhaps if you didn’t spend your last check on imported bourbon and catnip...LLOYD > The catnip was for you.LUNA > And I appreciated the gesture. But appreciation doesn’t buy tuna. Wait.
Luna’s ears swivel toward the frosted glass door. Her tail twitches—a metronome of agitation.
LUNA (CONT'D) > Do you smell that? Jasmine. And... fear. High-grade, expensive fear.
A silhouette darkens the glass. A KNOCK, hesitant but firm.
LLOYD > Showtime.
ACT ONE
SCENE 2
INT. LLOYD SCHWARZER’S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS
The door opens. LADY MARGAUX (30s) enters. She moves like liquid smoke. She wears a burgundy dress that hugs her curves like a secret. She is devastatingly beautiful, but her eyes are shattered glass.
Lloyd stands, buttoning his jacket, smoothing the wrinkles.
MARGAUX > (French accent, thick as cream) > Detective Schwarzer? I was told you are the man one sees when the police stop listening.LLOYD > The police usually stop listening when I start talking. Please, sit.
Margaux takes the client chair. She doesn’t relax; she perches.
MARGAUX > Someone is trying to kill me, Detective.LLOYD > That’s a heavy opener. Usually, people start with the weather. Who wants you dead, Lady Margaux?MARGAUX > If I knew, I would not be in this... quaint office.LUNA > (To Lloyd only) > She’s lying. Or holding back. Smell the adrenaline? It’s familiar. Like the night the pier burned down.
Lloyd ignores the cat, leaning in, opting for professionalism mixed with charm.
LLOYD > Walk me through it.MARGAUX > It started three weeks ago. Letters. No return address. Just words cut from magazines. *“Your beauty is a borrowed thing.”* Then, yesterday...
Her breath hitches. She pulls a silk handkerchief from her clutch.
MARGAUX (CONT'D) > I was in the foyer of my estate. The crystal chandelier—it has hung there for fifty years. It fell. It missed me by inches. The shards... they cut my cheek.
She turns her head. A thin, red scratch mars her perfect skin.
LLOYD > Gravity is rarely that malicious on its own.MARGAUX > My husband passed last year. A tragic boating accident. Since then, I am alone in that house. I hear footsteps in the walls, Detective. I am rich, I am beautiful, and I am terrified.LLOYD > I’ll take the case. But my rates are—MARGAUX > (Slides a thick envelope across the desk) > Triple whatever you are thinking. Just keep me alive.
Margaux stands. The scent of jasmine floods the room. She looks at Lloyd, a spark of hope in the emerald depths of her eyes.
MARGAUX (CONT'D) > Do not make me regret this, Mr. Schwarzer.
She leaves. The room feels colder without her.
LUNA > She’s trouble, Lloyd. The kind that wears diamonds to a funeral.LLOYD > (Pocketing the envelope) > She’s a client, Luna. And tonight, we eat the good tuna.
SCENE 3
INT. POLICE PRECINCT - BULLPEN - NIGHT
The precinct is a hive of controlled chaos. Phones ringing, drunks shouting. Lloyd navigates the maze of desks to find DETECTIVE ASHLEY MARTINEZ (30s). She’s sharp, tired, and wearing a leather jacket that means business. She’s staring at a murder board.
ASHLEY > You’re not supposed to be back here, Lloyd. Crawford sees you, he’ll have an aneurysm.LLOYD > I worry about his blood pressure daily. I need intel, Ash.
Ashley sighs, turning to him. There’s history here. A lot of it.
ASHLEY > We found another one yesterday. Down by the docks. Brunette. Late twenties.LLOYD > Same M.O.?ASHLEY > Mostly. Strangled. But look at this.
She taps a photo on the board. A close-up of the victim's neck.
ASHLEY (CONT'D) > Distinctive knot pattern. A double fisherman’s loop. And she was stripped of her jewelry. Specifically, a ruby necklace.
Lloyd’s mind races. Margaux was wearing rubies.
LLOYD > A thief with a murder fetish?ASHLEY > Or a murderer who likes trophies. We found a witness who saw a figure in a hoodie near the scene. But get this—the attacks only happen on weekends.LLOYD > Weekend warrior. I have a client, Ash. High profile. She fits the victimology.ASHLEY > (Eyebrow raised) > The French aristocrat? I saw her file a report about a "clumsy chandelier." Careful, Lloyd. That woman has buried two husbands.LLOYD > Jealous?ASHLEY > Worried. You have a habit of falling for the broken ones.
SCENE 4
INT. PRECINCT BREAK ROOM - MOMENTS LATER
The vending machine hums loudly. The fluorescent light flickers. It’s the most romantic spot in the building. Lloyd hands Ashley a terrible paper cup of coffee.
LLOYD > To keeping the streets safe.ASHLEY > (Taking a sip, grimacing) > This tastes like battery acid.LLOYD > It’s an acquired taste. Like me.
Ashley leans against the counter, softening. The distance between them shrinks.
ASHLEY > How have you been, really? Since... the hearing?LLOYD > Surviving. Luna keeps me humble.ASHLEY > You still talk to that cat?LLOYD > She talks to me. There’s a difference.
Ashley laughs, a warm sound that cuts through the precinct noise. She reaches out, adjusting his lapel.
ASHLEY > You’re a good detective, Lloyd. Even without the badge. Just... don’t get dead. I’d hate to have to do the paperwork.LLOYD > (Softly) > I’ll try to keep my paperwork to a minimum.
The moment is charged. Almost a kiss. Then—
RADIO DISPATCH (O.S.)
All units, 211 in progress. Jewelry store on 5th and Main. Suspect fleeing on foot, headed East.
Ashley snaps back to reality.
ASHLEY > That’s two blocks away.LLOYD > (Grinning) > Race you?
ACT TWO
SCENE 5
EXT. CITY STREETS / ALLEYWAY - NIGHT
Rain slicked pavement reflects the neon signs. Lloyd and Ashley are sprinting. Lloyd isn't as fast as he used to be, but he knows the shortcuts.
He veers off into a narrow alley, vaulting over a dumpster.
LLOYD > (Panting) > If I have a heart attack... tell Luna... she’s adopted!
He bursts out onto the next street, cutting off a HOODED FIGURE clutching a bag.
The Figure sees Lloyd and pulls a KNIFE.
LLOYD (CONT'D) > Woah there, Zorro. You really want to bring a knife to a—
Ashley slides into the frame, gun drawn.
ASHLEY > —Gun fight? Drop it!
The Thief looks between Lloyd and Ashley. He throws the bag at Lloyd’s face and SCRAMBLES up a fire escape with inhuman agility.
Lloyd catches the bag. Rubies spill out.
LLOYD > He’s a gymnast. Great.
SCENE 6
EXT. STREET - MOMENTS LATER
Police cruisers screech to a halt. CAPTAIN CRAWFORD (50s, looks like a bulldog chewing a wasp) storms out.
CRAWFORD > Martinez! What in God’s name are you doing running ops with a civilian?ASHLEY > He was a witness, Captain. He intervened.CRAWFORD > He’s a liability! Schwarzer, if I see you near my crime scenes again, I’ll lock you up for obstruction so fast your head will spin.
Lloyd steps in front of Ashley.
LLOYD > Save the speech, Crawford. We recovered the stolen goods. And your suspect is heading toward the train yards. If you stop yelling and start driving, you might catch him.
Crawford turns purple. He glares at Ashley.
CRAWFORD > My office. Tomorrow. 0800.
Crawford stomps off. Ashley looks at Lloyd.
ASHLEY > You just made my life hell for the next week.LLOYD > I got you the lead. Besides, he hates me anyway. Might as well earn it.ASHLEY > (Suppressed smile) > Get out of here, Lloyd. Before I arrest you myself.
ACT THREE
SCENE 7
EXT. ART GALLERY - NIGHT
Lloyd walks home, adrenaline fading. He passes a high-end gallery opening. People in tuxedos spill onto the sidewalk.
Suddenly, a man bumps into him hard. Lloyd stumbles.
LLOYD > Easy, pal. The sidewalk is wide enough for—
The man turns. This is LUKE (30s). Disheveled hair, paint-stained fingers, wearing a tuxedo that looks slept in. He has an intense, brooding energy.
LUKE > Chaos cannot be contained by sidewalks, my friend.
Luke stares at a mural on the wall of the gallery—a chaotic splash of red and black.
LUKE (CONT'D) > What do you see?
Lloyd looks at the mural. He’s tired. He wants a drink.
LLOYD > I see a mess.LUKE > (Laughs) > Precisely. It’s a tragedy. That is the only honest art form left. The tragedy of the inevitable falling apart of things.
Luke looks at Lloyd, his gaze piercing.
LUKE (CONT'D) > You have the eyes of a man who watches things break. A detective?LLOYD > A private one.LUKE > Ah. The mercenary of truth. I am Luke. I paint what people don’t want to see.LLOYD > Lloyd. I find what people don’t want found.LUKE > We should drink to that. The seekers of uncomfortable things.
Neon lights from the gallery spill onto the wet pavement. LLOYD faces LUKE, who is sipping from a silver flask like he owns the night.
LLOYD > Burgundy dress? Where?LUKE > Near the old clock tower. She was meeting someone.LLOYD > Who?LUKE > (Smirks, pocketing the flask) > A ghost, perhaps? Or just bad news in a cheap suit.LLOYD > Why are you telling me this? You hate helpfulness. It gives you hives.LUKE > Because watching you play detective is like watching a dog try to do algebra. Besides... chaos is more fun when there are players on the board.
Luke turns and vanishes into the gallery crowd—a phantom in a tuxedo.
Lloyd stands alone. His phone vibrates. Caller ID: MARGAUX.
He answers immediately.
LLOYD > Margaux?
Silence on the line. Then, jagged, panicked breathing.
MARGAUX (V.O.) > (Whispering) > *He’s here, Lloyd. He found me.*LLOYD > Margaux! Lock the door. Margaux!
CLICK. The line goes dead.
Lloyd stares at the phone. His eyes go hard. He sprints toward his car.
FADE OUT.


EPISODE 2: HOUSEHOLD SECRETS
TEASER
SCENE 1 EXT. CHATEAU MARGAUX - DAY
A Gothic monstrosity of grey stone and gargoyles looms against a bruised purple sky. It screams "old money" and "bad luck."
LLOYD SCHWARZER’S beat-up sedan ROARS up the gravel driveway, drifting around a curve. This isn't a casual visit; he’s driving like he’s escaping a burning building.
He screeches to a halt. Lloyd jumps out, hand hovering near the concealed holster under his jacket.
LUNA (O.S.) > You drive like a suicidal lemur.
Lloyd looks down. LUNA (the cat) is sitting calmly on the dashboard, looking unbothered.
LLOYD > She said "He’s here," Luna. That doesn't imply a tea party.LUNA > (Stretching) > It implies a mouse in the pantry. Or a murderer. Let’s hope for the mouse. I’m hungry.
Lloyd rushes the massive oak front doors. Before he can touch the handle, a shadow eclipses the sun.
BORIS (50s) blocks the path. He is built like a Soviet tank, uniform immaculate, eyes dead. He smells of gasoline and secrets. He doesn't move.
BORIS > Deliveries are in the rear.LLOYD > (Step forward, intense) > Get out of my way, Boris. Lady Margaux is in danger.BORIS > (Unblinking) > Madame is resting. She is not to be disturbed by... stray animals.
Boris looks pointedly at Lloyd, then down at Luna.
LLOYD > I’m not asking for permission. I’m giving you a warning. Move. Or I move you.
Boris stares at him. For a second, violence hangs in the air like humidity. Then, Boris steps aside, slowly. A predator letting prey pass.
BORIS > The Solarium. Wipe your feet. Twice.
Lloyd brushes past him.
LUNA > (To Lloyd as they enter) > I don't like him. He smells like industrial cleaner... and bitterness.
ACT ONE
SCENE 2 INT. MANSION - SOLARIUM - CONTINUOUS
Glass walls. Exotic plants. The air is humid and smells of expensive perfume and rotting orchids.
LADY MARGAUX sits on a chaise lounge. She looks stunning but shattered—pale skin, trembling hands.
Two younger people are arguing near the window. THIERRY (21), handsome but twitchy, and CELESTE (22), holding a sketchbook with a "burn it all down" attitude.
THIERRY > Maman, you’re hallucinating again! There was no one in the hallway!CELESTE > (Sketching furiously) > Just because *you* were passed out doesn't mean she didn't see him, Thierry.MARGAUX > (Voice shaking) > I know what I saw. A shadow. By the bedroom door.
Lloyd bursts in. The energy in the room shifts. Margaux looks up, her eyes locking onto his. A wave of visible relief washes over her face.
MARGAUX > Lloyd.THIERRY > Great. The noir cliché is here.LLOYD > (Ignoring Thierry, walking straight to Margaux) > You called. You said he found you.
Lloyd kneels beside her chaise. He’s close. Intimately close. He takes her hands in his. Her fingers are ice cold; his are warm.
MARGAUX > (Breathless) > He was outside the door. I heard the handle turn. When I screamed... he ran.LLOYD > (Low, intense) > Did you see his face?MARGAUX > No. Just the dark. But I felt it, Lloyd. The malice.
Lloyd squeezes her hands. The look between them is heavy—protective on his side, desperate on hers. It lingers a beat too long for "just business."
CELESTE > (Dryly) > Hate to interrupt the romance novel cover shoot, but we have security. Boris checked the grounds. Nobody’s here.
Lloyd stands up, unwillingly breaking the contact.
LLOYD > Boris wouldn't find a polar bear in a snowstorm if he didn't want to. I’m staying until I clear the house myself.THIERRY > We don't need a babysitter. Especially one who drives a car held together by duct tape.LLOYD > (Cold smile) > I’m cheaper than a funeral, Thierry. And less depressing.
ANABELLE (28), a maid who seems to be made of silence and grey fabric, enters with a silver tea service. She keeps her head down.
ANABELLE > Your tea, Madame. Chamomile. To calm the nerves.
Anabelle pours a cup. Her hands tremble.
SCENE 3 INT. SOLARIUM - CONTINUOUS
Margaux sighs, leaning back, her eyes still drifting toward Lloyd.
MARGAUX > Thank you, Anabelle. You’re the only one who doesn't treat me like I’m insane.
Margaux lifts the delicate porcelain cup.
Luna, sitting by Lloyd’s feet, suddenly goes rigid. Her ears flatten.
LUNA > *Lloyd. The smell. Bitter almonds. Don't let her drink that!*LLOYD > (Instinctively) > Don't—!
Lloyd lunges. He doesn't just slap the cup; he tackles the situation. He grabs Margaux’s wrist and jerks it away, pulling her slightly toward him.
The cup flies. It shatters against a marble planter. The liquid splashes onto a delicate fern.
SSSZZT.
The fern hisses. Smoke rises. The green leaves turn black and shrivel instantly.
Silence. Absolute, terrified silence.
THIERRY > What the hell...LLOYD > (Staring at the dying plant) > That’s a hell of a kick for chamomile.
Lloyd is still leaning over Margaux, his hand gripping her shoulder, shielding her body with his own. He looks down at her. Her chest is heaving.
LLOYD (CONT'D) > (Softly) > You okay?MARGAUX > (Wide-eyed, staring at his lips) > You... you saved me. Again.LLOYD > Habit.
He helps her sit up. The air is thick enough to cut with a knife. He forces himself to step back, turning to the room.
LLOYD (CONT'D) > Nobody leaves this room.
ACT TWO
SCENE 4 INT. SOLARIUM - MOMENTS LATER
Lloyd has secured the doors. He’s crouching by the spill.
LUNA > (Sniffing from a safe distance) > Arsenic base. Highly concentrated. It wasn't in the pot, Lloyd. It was in the cup.
Lloyd stands, turning on Anabelle. She is shaking so hard her teeth chatter.
LLOYD > You poured it.ANABELLE > (Weeping) > I took the cup from the cupboard! It was clean! I swear on my mother’s life!LLOYD > Who else has been in the kitchen today?ANABELLE > Everyone! Mademoiselle Celeste paints in there. Monsieur Thierry gets ice for his... bruises.LLOYD > Anyone else? Think.ANABELLE > (Hesitant) > Boris.
Lloyd freezes.
LLOYD > The chauffeur? Why was he in the kitchen?ANABELLE > He said he was... checking the pipes under the sink. Where we keep the cleaning supplies.CELESTE > (Closing her sketchbook) > Boris fixing pipes? Please. The man thinks plumbing is a communist plot.LLOYD > (To Margaux) > He tried to stop me from coming in. He stalled me at the door. Gave the poison time to work.
Margaux looks ill. She reaches for Lloyd’s hand again, needing the anchor.
MARGAUX > Not Boris. He’s been with us for twenty years.LLOYD > (Squeezing her hand) > Twenty years is a long time to bury a grudge, Margaux.
Lloyd signals to Luna. They move to the corner, away from the family.
LLOYD (CONT'D) > (Whispering) > Assessment?LUNA > The maid smells like terror. The boy smells like sweat and gambling debt. The girl smells like turpentine and daddy issues. But the poison... that didn't come from inside the house.LLOYD > Explain.LUNA > There’s a trail. A very faint trail of that same chemical dust. It leads out the French doors. Into the garden.LLOYD > Stay with Margaux. If Boris comes in here, hiss. If he tries anything, aim for the jugular.LUNA > With pleasure.
Lloyd checks his weapon and heads for the glass doors.
MARGAUX > Lloyd? Where are you going?LLOYD > (Looking back, a grim smile) > Gardening.
He slips out.
SCENE 5 EXT. GARDENS - CONTINUOUS
Lloyd steps out into the overgrown garden. It’s a maze of thorns, statues, and neglect. Luna bounds ahead, nose twitching.
LUNA > This way. Past the hydrangeas. Nature is just a salad that wants to kill you, Lloyd. Watch the thorns.
Lloyd follows. The path leads away from the manicured lawns toward the edge of the property, where the woods begin. The Chateau looms behind them like a grey tombstone.
LLOYD > You smell the poison trail?LUNA > Faintly. Mixed with... stale tobacco. And sweat. Someone has been walking this path often.
They arrive at a dilapidated gardener’s shed. The wood is rotting; the door hangs off one hinge.
LLOYD > (Drawing his weapon) > Stay behind me.LUNA > (Sitting down) > Obviously. I have nine lives, but I’d prefer to keep the current one. It has good naps.
Lloyd kicks the door open.
INT. GARDEN SHED - CONTINUOUS
It’s not a tool shed anymore. It’s a nest.
A sleeping bag in the corner. Fast food wrappers. And on a makeshift table: Monitors.
Lloyd steps closer. The monitors show live feeds of the mansion. The Solarium. The Kitchen. Margaux’s Bedroom.
LLOYD > Wireless cameras. High tech. This isn't a jealous ex-husband or a peeping tom. This is a pro.LUNA > Lloyd. Look at the wall.
On the rotting wood, photos are tacked up. Dozens of them. All of Margaux. Candid shots. Sleeping. Eating. Laughing.
But her eyes in every photo are scratched out with violent red marker.
Suddenly—a SNAP of a twig outside.
ACT THREE
SCENE 6 EXT. WOODS - CONTINUOUS
Lloyd spins around and bursts out of the shed.
A FIGURE in a dark hoodie is sprinting toward the treeline.
LLOYD > Hey! Police! Or close enough!
Lloyd gives chase. He vaults a fallen log, splashing through mud.
The Figure is fast. Agile. They scramble up a ridge leading to the back road.
Lloyd pushes harder, lungs burning. He reaches the ridge just as an ENGINE ROARS.
A grey sedan peels out, tires spitting gravel. Lloyd catches a partial plate: 7-G-K...
He stops, hands on knees, panting.
LLOYD > (To himself) > I really need to stop smoking. Or start running.
Luna trots up, looking unbothered.
LUNA > You run like a duck, Lloyd. A very determined duck.LLOYD > (Checking the ground) > Did you see the driver?LUNA > No. But look.
She paws at the mud where the car was parked. There’s a cigarette butt.
LUNA (CONT'D) > Clove cigarettes. Distinctive. Hipster poison.
Lloyd picks it up with a handkerchief. He stands, turning back toward the house.
Standing at the edge of the garden, watching them, is BORIS.
He’s too far away to have been the runner. But he’s standing perfectly still, arms crossed, staring at the dust cloud the car left behind. He doesn't look surprised.
LLOYD > (Muttering) > Tell me he doesn't look like he just supervised a shift change.
SCENE 7 EXT. MANSION DRIVEWAY - EVENING
Lloyd walks back to the main house. A police cruiser is already there. CAPTAIN CRAWFORD (50s) leans against it, looking smug.
CRAWFORD > Schwarzer. I heard a radio call about a disturbance. You playing hero or villain today?LLOYD > I’m playing janitor, Crawford. Cleaning up the mess you haven't noticed yet. Someone just tried to poison Lady Margaux.
Crawford straightens up, the smirk vanishing.
CRAWFORD > Poison? Is she dead?LLOYD > No thanks to your patrol routes. I found a surveillance nest in the shed. Suspect fled in a grey sedan.
Lloyd tosses the evidence bag with the cigarette butt to Crawford.
LLOYD (CONT'D) > Run prints on that. And check the perimeter for wireless transmitters.
Crawford catches the bag. He looks at Lloyd with grudging respect, then over Lloyd’s shoulder.
Boris is there, polishing the chrome on the family limo, listening.
CRAWFORD > You’re doing my job, Schwarzer.LLOYD > Someone has to. Just keep a unit here tonight.CRAWFORD > (Leaning in) > You think it’s the chauffeur?LLOYD > I think the chauffeur knows more about wireless cameras than a man who waxes cars should. Watch him.
SCENE 8 INT. MANSION - FOYER - NIGHT
Lloyd prepares to leave. The house is quiet now.
Margaux descends the stairs. She’s changed into a silk robe that clings to her frame. She looks like a ghost of the woman from the Solarium—haunted, but beautiful.
MARGAUX > Crawford said the man got away.LLOYD > For now. But he left crumbs. I’m good with crumbs.
Margaux steps into his personal space. The air shifts again—electric. She reaches out, her hand resting flat against his chest, right over his heart.
MARGAUX > (Whispering) > Stay. Please. I don't feel safe unless you’re in the room.
Lloyd looks down at her. He covers her hand with his own. For a second, he wants to say yes.
LLOYD > If I stay, I’m just a bodyguard. If I go, I can find the guy who wants to hurt you. I can't do both.MARGAUX > (Her thumb stroking his lapel) > You could come back. After.LLOYD > (Voice rough) > Count on it. Lock the doors, Margaux. Trust no one. Not even the people you gave birth to. And definitely not the man driving your car.
He gently moves her hand away, though it clearly pains him to do it.
He opens the door.
INT. LLOYD'S CAR - MOMENTS LATER
Lloyd slumps into the driver's seat. He exhales a long breath, staring at the dark house.
Luna hops into the passenger side.
LUNA > You have lipstick on your collar. Metaphorically speaking.LLOYD > Fear makes people do strange things, Luna. Don't read into it.
He starts the engine. It coughs, then roars to life.
LUNA > Lloyd?LLOYD > Yeah?LUNA > That scent on the cigarette butt... the clove?LLOYD > What about it?LUNA > It was under the smell of smoke. But there was something else on it. Stronger.LLOYD > Spit it out.LUNA > Paint thinner. And turpentine. High grade.
Lloyd freezes, hand on the gear shift. His eyes narrow.
LLOYD > Turpentine?LUNA > And cheap cologne. The kind that tries to cover up the smell of... solvents.
Lloyd thinks of the gallery. He thinks of Luke. And he thinks of the "Artist" vibe of the gallery opening.
LLOYD > The artist.LUNA > The tragedy of things falling apart usually starts with a paintbrush.
Lloyd shifts gears.
LLOYD > Let’s go pay a visit to a studio.
He slams on the gas. The car speeds away into the night.
FADE OUT.
END OF EPISODE 2


EPISODE 3: BLOOD AND JASMINE
TEASER
SCENE 1
EXT. ALLEYWAY - NIGHT
Rain lashes against the pavement, washing away the grime of the city but not the sins. Police tape flutters in the wind.
LLOYD SCHWARZER ducks under the tape. He wears a trench coat that has seen better decades.
DETECTIVE ASHLEY MARTINEZ is kneeling beside a body covered by a tarp. She looks up, rain dripping from her hair. She looks exhausted and beautiful.
ASHLEY > You have a radar for dead bodies, Lloyd. Or do you just follow the sirens?LLOYD > I follow the smell of trouble. And cheap coffee. You look tired, Ash.ASHLEY > (Standing up) > Third one this month. Look.
She pulls back the tarp. The victim is a brunette. Striking features. She wears a torn evening gown and ruby-red lipstick. The scent of jasmine is overpowering, mixing with the metallic tang of blood.
LLOYD > Jasmine perfume. Ruby lipstick. She looks...ASHLEY > Like your client. Like a discount version of Lady Margaux.
SERGEANT CRAWFORD (50s) stomps over, barking orders at a uniform. He spots Lloyd.
CRAWFORD > Schwarzer! This is a crime scene, not a cocktail party. Get lost.LLOYD > Nice to see you too, Crawford. You missed a spot shaving.CRAWFORD > (Step closer, aggressive) > You think this is a game? We have a serial killer hunting women who fit a profile. If you’re withholding evidence from your rich girlfriend, I’ll have your license.LLOYD > (Coolly) > If I had evidence, you’d be the second to know. Right after Ashley solves the case for you.
Crawford turns purple. Ashley steps between them, her hand brushing Lloyd’s arm—a professional touch that lingers a second too long.
ASHLEY > (Softly, to Lloyd) > Go, Lloyd. I’ll text you the coroner’s report. Just... watch your back.
Lloyd catches her eye. There's heat there.
LLOYD > Only if you watch my front.
ACT ONE
The vintage Rolls Royce sits gleaming under the fluorescent lights.
Lloyd enters, adjusting his tuxedo. He stops.
CELESTE (22) is sitting on the hood of the Rolls Royce. She’s wearing a stunning black evening dress, but she’s ruined the effect by wearing heavy combat boots.
She is sharpening a charcoal pencil with a wicked-looking silver box cutter. Scritch. Scritch. The sound is rhythmic and sharp.
CELESTE > You look uncomfortable, Detective. Like a dog in a sweater.LLOYD > It’s called a tuxedo, Celeste. And get off the car. Boris will have a stroke.
Celeste hops off. She circles Lloyd, the box cutter still in her hand. She moves with a predatory grace, very different from her mother’s elegance. This is raw heat.
CELESTE > (Smirking) > You’re really enjoying this, aren't you? Playing the white knight for my mother?
She stops in front of him, way too close. She reaches out with her free hand and straightens his bowtie. Her fingers linger on his neck. Her eyes drop to his lips, then back up.
LLOYD > I’m doing a job.CELESTE > Is that what she calls it?
She presses her body slightly against his. It’s inappropriate, dangerous, and undeniable. Lloyd doesn't step back.
CELESTE (CONT'D) > You know, I’m the one who really sees things in this house. Mom is blind. Thierry is stupid. But you... you’re interesting.LLOYD > (Catching her wrist holding the knife) > And you’re playing with sharp objects.
Celeste laughs, low and throaty. She pulls her hand free, retracting the blade with a loud CLICK.
CELESTE > Careful, Lloyd. You might cut yourself on all this edge.
She grabs her sketchbook and strdes out of the garage, leaving a trail of tension behind her.
ACT ONE
SCENE 3
INT. MANSION - MARGAUX'S DRESSING ROOM - EVENING
Luxury screams from every corner. LADY MARGAUX stands before a tri-fold mirror, wearing a backless emerald gown that fits like a second skin.
